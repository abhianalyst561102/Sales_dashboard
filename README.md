# Sales_dashboard
I have created a sales dashboard on US Superstore dataset
1. Firstly, Inserted a slicer to filter the data on the year basis
2. Created some cards , where I have shown Total sales in million, Total profit in thousand and Total quantity sold in thousand
3. Created a donut chart to show the sales region wise
4. Inserted a pie chart to view the sales by segments as there are three segments which are "Consumer, Corporate, Home Office", The maximum sale is coming from the Consumer segment.
5. Inserted a globe to show the sales by state , used bubble which means where the bubble is big that means there is sale is also greater
6. To focus on vip customer , I have inserted stacked column chart where I have shown 5 who is contributing most in the profit
7.   To focus on profitable products, I have inserted a clustered bar chart and filtered the chart by top 5 products on profit basis.
